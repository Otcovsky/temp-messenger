Features

Everything you need to talk — and nothing extra about you

Temp Messenger is designed to be disposable: conversations belong to the devices of the people talking, not to a server.

No phone number or email

Your identity is a key pair your device creates in a second. No one learns your phone number, and there’s no one to hand it over.

End-to-end encryption

X25519, AES-256-GCM and Double Ratchet: every message has its own key. Only the other person can read it.

The server keeps nothing

It only relays ciphertext and doesn’t know who the sender is. Undelivered messages wait up to 7 days and are deleted as soon as they’re picked up.

Audio and video calls

One-to-one calls, encrypted too: voice and video go directly between devices or through a server that can’t decrypt them.

Files up to 25 MB

Photos and documents are encrypted with a one-time key. As soon as the other person downloads a file, it’s erased from the server.

Group chats

Every group message is encrypted separately for each member. The server has no member list.

How it works

Your first message in a minute

  1. Pick a name

    Open the chat and enter the name others will see. Encryption keys are created right on your device.

  2. Send an invitation

    Share a link any way you like or show a QR code. The key for the first handshake lives inside the link itself and never reaches the server.

  3. Talk

    Write, call, send files. Want to disappear? Clear the data and not a trace of the conversation remains.

The honest part: there’s no recovery

No account means nothing to recover. If you clear site data, uninstall the app or lose your phone, your contacts and conversations are gone for good. That’s not a flaw — it’s the whole idea: what isn’t stored anywhere can’t be stolen or demanded.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a phone number or email?

No. You just pick a name — that’s what the people you talk to will see. Your identity is a key pair your device creates on its own.

What does the server see?

Only encrypted packets addressed to random mailboxes. It doesn’t see text, names, files or who sent them. Undelivered messages are kept for up to 7 days and deleted as soon as they’re picked up. No access logs with IP addresses are kept.

Where are my conversations stored?

Only on your device: in the browser’s storage or in the app’s memory. There are no backups on the server.

Can I recover my conversations?

No. If you clear site data, uninstall the app or lose your phone, the keys and conversations are gone for good. There’s nothing to recover and no one to recover it from.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, through Safari: open chat.tempmessenger.tech and add it to your Home Screen. A dedicated iPhone app is planned.

Why an APK and not Google Play?

The app is being prepared for the stores. For now you can install it as a file: Android will ask once to allow installs from this source.

Is this a finished version?

No, it’s an early beta. The essentials already work: messages, files, groups, calls. But expect bugs, and features will keep improving and growing.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. There’s no account, so there’s no subscription either.
